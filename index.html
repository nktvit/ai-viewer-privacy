Privacy Policy — Illustrator AI File Viewer

Last updated: 26 November 2026

"Illustrator AI File Viewer" (package com.vitostools.aiviewer, the "app") is an Android app by Vitos Tools for viewing Adobe Illustrator (.ai) files. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. We run no servers for the app, and it contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your files

Files you choose to open are read, rendered, exported and printed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. When you export or share a file, it goes only where you send it, using Android's own share, save and print dialogs.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a few settings locally on your device: a list of recently opened files (names and locations), your theme and default export format, and the folder you picked for saving exports. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Permissions

  • Files / storage access: used only to open the .ai files you select and to save exports to the location you choose.
  • Internet: the app does not send your data over the network. Print jobs are handled by Android's print service, and links you tap (such as this policy) open in your browser.

Purchases

The app offers an optional paid upgrade (Pro) as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases, free trials and billing are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether your subscription is active.

Third parties

We do not share data with any third party. Google Play may collect its own information when you install the app, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
